Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what data Sheet Music Analysis ("we") receives, why we use it and how long we keep it. The service has no user accounts and no advertising.

What we receive

In Claude

When you choose your score file in the card in Claude, it is sent straight to our server. When you give Claude a link to your score instead, our server downloads the file from that link. Either way we use the file and store it as described for ChatGPT below. We do not keep the link.

In ChatGPT

The score you attach is sent to our server. We use it to read the music (for a PDF), draft the analysis and draw the annotated pages. We store the score, the music read from a PDF, the draft and the drawn pages so the conversation can refer back to them.

What we do not receive

We do not receive your chat messages, name, email address or account details. With each request ChatGPT also sends an anonymous user ID, an anonymous conversation ID and your language setting. We do not store any of these as sent. From the two IDs we make the pseudonymous identifiers described below; we do not use the language setting.

A pseudonymous identifier is a code made from ChatGPT's ID with a secret key held on our server. The same ID always gives the same code. Without the key the code cannot be turned back into the ID, and we have no way to link it to your name or account.

How we use it

We use this data only to provide the service, keep it within its capacity, fix problems and measure usage. We do not sell it, share it with anyone other than the service providers below, use it for advertising, or use it to train models.

Service providers

  • Google Cloud (United States) runs the analysis server and stores the files and logs above.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and our domain, and forwards requests to the analysis server without storing them. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@sheetmusicanalysis.com. We do not know who sent a given file, so include the date and the name of the piece. We cannot link a usage identifier to a person, so we cannot find one person's identifier on request.

The service is meant for general audiences, including students under 18. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
