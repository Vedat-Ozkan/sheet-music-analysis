| `amazing-grace-lead-sheet.musicxml` | A lead sheet written by hand for these tests: the melody of the traditional hymn tune "New Britain" (first printed 1829, public domain), as sung to "Amazing Grace", in G major, 3/4, with a pickup bar and the chord symbols a lead sheet usually prints over it (G, G7, C, Em, D, D7; two symbols in bar 13). No lyrics, no arranger. Written for this suite because the repo had no score with real chord symbols, and the dataset rows checked for one either credit a modern arranger or are modern songs. |
